## Basis
1.4.
The tests shall be made on the points of the tank which are regarded as
vulnerable to frontal or rear collisions. The points regarded as vulnerable are
those which are most exposed or weakest having regard to the shape of the
tank or the way in which it is installed on the vehicle. The points selected by
the laboratories shall be indicated in the test report.
