# S5.3.2.1 Force a 19 mm ( 3/4 inch) diameter cylindrical steel plunger with a hemispherical end perpendicularly into the 

## Statement
S5.3.2.1 Force a 19 mm ( 3/4 inch) diameter cylindrical steel plunger with a hemispherical end perpendicularly into the tread rib as near to the centerline as possible, avoiding penetration into the tread groove, at the rate of 50 mm (2 inches) per minute.
