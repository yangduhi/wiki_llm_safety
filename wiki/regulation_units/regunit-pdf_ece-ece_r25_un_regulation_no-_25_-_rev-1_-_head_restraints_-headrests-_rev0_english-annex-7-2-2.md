## Basis
2.2.
"Head restraint" means a device whose function is to limit the
rearward displacement of an adult occupant's head in relation to
his torso in order to reduce the danger of injury to the cervical
vertebrae of that occupant in the event of an accident;
1/
The head restraints of category M1 vehicles which conform to the
provisions of Regulation No. 17 "Uniform provisions concerning the approval of
vehicles with regard to the strength of seats and their anchorages, and
characteristics of any head restraints provided for the seats", are not
required to conform to the provisions of this Regulation.
E/ECE/324 Rev.1/Add.24/Rev.1
