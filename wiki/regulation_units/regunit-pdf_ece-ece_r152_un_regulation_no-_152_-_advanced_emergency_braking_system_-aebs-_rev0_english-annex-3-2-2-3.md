## Basis
2.2.
The subject vehicle shall travel in a straight line for a distance of at least 60 m.
at a constant speed in the range of speeds listed in the Table of
paragraph 5.2.2.4. to pass the stationary pedestrian target.
During the test there shall be no adjustment of any subject vehicle control other
than slight steering adjustments to counteract any drifting.
