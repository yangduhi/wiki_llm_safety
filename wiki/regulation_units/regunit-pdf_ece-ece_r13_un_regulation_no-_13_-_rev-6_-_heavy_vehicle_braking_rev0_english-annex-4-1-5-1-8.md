## Basis
1.5.1.8.
For vehicles equipped with an electric regenerative braking system of category B,
the condition of the vehicle batteries at the start of the test, shall be such that the
braking force contribution provided by the electric regenerative braking system
does not exceed the minimum guaranteed by the system design.
This requirement is deemed to be satisfied if the batteries are at one of the state of
charge conditions listed in the fourth clause of paragraph 1.4.1.2.2. above.
