## Basis
2.15.
"Shoulder median plane" means a plane dividing the left or right (as
applicable) shoulder clevis into symmetrical anterior/posterior sections. The
shoulder median plane is perpendicular to the centreline of the shoulder pivot
shaft and parallel to the shoulder load cell y-axis (or an equivalently oriented
axis of a shoulder load cell structural replacement).
