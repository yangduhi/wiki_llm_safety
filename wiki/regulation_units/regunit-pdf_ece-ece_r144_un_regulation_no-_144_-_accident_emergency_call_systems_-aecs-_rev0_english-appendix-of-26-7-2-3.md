## Basis
26.7.2.3.
After the impact test under UN Regulations Nos. 94 and/or 95 whichever is
relevant, the AECS back-up power supply shall be able to supply power to
the AECS. This may be verified by one of the methods described in Annex
11 to this Regulation.
