## Basis
9.2.1.1.
Five Child Restraint Systems shall be subjected to the dynamic test described
in paragraph 7.1.3. above. The Technical Service that conducted the type
approval tests shall choose the conditions that produced the maximum
horizontal head excursion during the type approval dynamic tests, excluding
the conditions described in paragraph 6.6.4.1.6.2. above. All the five Child
Restraint Systems shall be tested under the same conditions.
