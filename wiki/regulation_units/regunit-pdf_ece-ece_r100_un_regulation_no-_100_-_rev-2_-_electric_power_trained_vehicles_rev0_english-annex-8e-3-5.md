## Basis
3.5.
During phase C of the test, the pan shall be covered by a screen. The screen
shall be placed 3 cm +/- 1 cm above the fuel level measured prior to the
ignition of the fuel. The screen shall be made of a refractory material, as
prescribed in Annex 8E - Appendix 1. There shall be no gap between the
bricks and they shall be supported over the fuel pan in such a manner that the
holes in the bricks are not obstructed. The length and width of the frame shall
be 2 cm to 4 cm smaller than the interior dimensions of the pan so that a gap
of 1 cm to 2 cm exists between the frame and the wall of the pan to allow
ventilation. Before the test the screen shall be at least at the ambient
temperature. The firebricks may be wetted in order to guarantee repeatable
test conditions.
