## Basis
7.13.
Apply a 100 N ± 10 N load to the back and pan assembly of the
3-D H machine at the intersection of the hip angle quadrant and the T-bar
housing. The direction of load application is maintained along a line passing
by the above intersection to a point just above the thigh bar housing (see
Figure 5-2 of Annex 5). Then carefully return the back pan to the seat back.
Care must be exercised throughout the remainder of the procedure to prevent
the 3-D H machine from sliding forward.
