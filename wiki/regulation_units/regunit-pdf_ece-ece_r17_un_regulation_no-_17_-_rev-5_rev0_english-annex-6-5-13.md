## Basis
5.13.
The head restraint and its anchorage shall be strong enough to bear without
breakage the load specified in paragraph 6.4.3.6. below. In the case of head
restraints integral with the seat-back, the requirements of this paragraph shall
apply to the part of the seat-back structure situated above a plane
perpendicular to the reference line at 540 mm from the R point.
