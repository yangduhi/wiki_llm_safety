## Basis
(i)
For a period from the impact until 30 minutes after the impact
there shall be no electrolyte spillage from the REESS into the
passenger compartment.
(ii)
No more than 7 per cent by volume of the REESS electrolyte
capacity shall spill from the REESS to the outside of the
passenger compartment (for open type traction batteries a
limitation to a maximum of 5 litres also applies).
