## Basis
1.1.
Pressure cycling test.
Five TPRD units undergo 11,000 internal pressure cycles with hydrogen gas
having gas quality compliant with ISO 14687-2/SAE J2719. The first five
pressure cycles are between 2 (±1) MPa and 150 per cent NWP (±1 MPa);
the remaining cycles are between 2 (±1) MPa and 125 per cent NWP (±1
MPa). The first 1,500 pressure cycles are conducted at a TPRD temperature
of 85 °C or higher. The remaining cycles are conducted at a TPRD
temperature of 55 (±5) °C. The maximum pressure cycling rate is ten cycles
per minute. Following this test, the pressure relief device shall comply the
requirements of Leak test (Annex 4, paragraph 1.8.), Flow rate test (Annex 4,
paragraph 1.10.) and Bench top activation test (Annex 4 paragraph 1.9.).
