## Basis
5.3.3.
When a vehicle passes from a low-adhesion surface (kL) to a high-adhesion
surface (kH) where kH > 0.5 and kH/kL > 2, 11/ with the full force 8/ applied on the
control device, the deceleration of the vehicle shall rise to the appropriate high
value within a reasonable time and the vehicle shall not deviate from its initial
course. The running speed and the instant of applying the brakes shall be so
calculated that, with the anti-lock system fully cycling on the low-adhesion
surface, the passage from one surface to the other occurs at approximately
50 km/h.
9/
The provisions of this paragraph apply as of 13 March 1992 (Decision of the Working
Party on the Construction of Vehicles, TRANS/SC.1/WP.29/341, para. 23).
10/ The purpose of these tests is to check that the wheels do not lock and that the vehicle
remains stable; it is not necessary, therefore, to make complete stops and bring the vehicle to a
halt on the low-adhesion surface.
11/
kH is the high-adhesion surface coefficient.
kL is the low-adhesion surface coefficient.
kH and kL are measured as laid down in Appendix 2 to this annex.
