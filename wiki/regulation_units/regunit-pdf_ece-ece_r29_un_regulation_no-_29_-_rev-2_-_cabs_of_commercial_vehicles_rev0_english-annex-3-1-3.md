## Basis
1.3.
Longitudinal attachment
Rearward movement of the chassis frame shall be limited by chains or ropes
A attached to the front of the chassis frame symmetrically in relation to its
longitudinal axis, the points of attachment being not less than 600 mm apart.
The chains or ropes shall when tensioned form a downward angle of not
more than 25° with the horizontal and their projection on a horizontal plane
shall form an angle of not more than 10° with the longitudinal axis of the
vehicle. The chains or ropes may cross one another.
