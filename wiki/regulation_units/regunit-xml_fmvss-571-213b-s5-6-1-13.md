# S5.6.1.13 For child restraints manufactured on or after January 8, 2028, in the case of child restraint systems marked a

## Statement
S5.6.1.13 For child restraints manufactured on or after January 8, 2028, in the case of child restraint systems marked as specified in S5.9(a) and (b) of this standard, explain that the markings identify the lower anchor connectors and the tether anchor connector, respectively, and that the consumer should look for corresponding marks on the vehicle child restraint anchorage system to attach the appropriate connectors of the child restraint system.
