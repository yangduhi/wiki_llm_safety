## Basis
2.5.
"ISOFIX" is a system that provides a method of connecting a Child Restraint
System to a vehicle. It is based on two vehicle anchorages and two
corresponding attachments on the Child Restraint System in conjunction with
a means to limit the pitch rotation of the Child Restraint System. All three
vehicle anchorages are to be approved according to Regulation No. 14.
