## Basis
4.5.
In the case of Child Restraint Systems that can be used forward facing, it
shall have the following label permanently attached and visible to the person
installing a child restraint in the vehicle:
The manufacturer shall be permitted to include the word "months" to
explain the symbol "M" in the label. The word "months" should be in a
language commonly spoken in the country or countries where the product is
sold. More than one language is allowed.
Minimum label size 40 x 40 mm
