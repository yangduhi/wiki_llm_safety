## Basis
4.5.
The stopping device assembly shall be maintained for at least 12 hours at a
temperature between 15 °C and 25 °C before being used for the calibration
tests described in Annex 7 to this Regulation. The stopping device shall, for
each type of test, meet the performance requirements laid down in Annex 7,
Appendices 1 and 2. For dynamic tests of a child restraint, the stopping
device assembly shall be maintained for at least 12 hours at the same
temperature, to within ±2 °C, as that of the calibration test. Any other device
giving equivalent results can be accepted.
