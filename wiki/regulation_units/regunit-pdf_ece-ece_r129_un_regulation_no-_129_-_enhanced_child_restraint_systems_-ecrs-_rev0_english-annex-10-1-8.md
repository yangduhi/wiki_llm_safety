## Basis
1.8.
The complete vehicle with the Child Restraint System installed in accordance
with the fitting instructions shall be placed on a hard, flat and level surface,
with the handbrake off and in neutral gear. More than one Child restraint
system may be tested in the same impact test.
1 The distance "a" is equal to the length of the synchronous pendulum under consideration.
