# S5.2 Adjustable steering controls are adjusted so that a tilting steering wheel hub is at the geometric center of the lo

## Statement
S5.2 Adjustable steering controls are adjusted so that a tilting steering wheel hub is at the geometric center of the locus it describes when it is moved through its full range of driving positions. A telescoping steering control is set at the adjustment position midway between the forwardmost and rearwardmost position.
