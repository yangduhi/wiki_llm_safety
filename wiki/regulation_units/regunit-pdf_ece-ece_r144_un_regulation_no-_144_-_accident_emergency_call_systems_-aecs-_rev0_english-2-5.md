## Basis
2.5.
"Airbag" means a device which, in the event of a severe impact affecting the
vehicle, automatically deploys a flexible structure intended to limit the
gravity of the contacts of one or more parts of the body of an occupant of the
vehicle with the interior of the passenger compartment.
