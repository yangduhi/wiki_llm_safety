## Basis
7.2.2.1.1.
When testing a manual adjusting device, the strap shall be drawn steadily
through the adjusting device, having regard for the normal conditions of use,
at a rate of 100 ± 20 mm/min and the maximum force measured to the nearest
integer value of N after the first 25 ± 5 mm of strap movement.
