## Basis
2.3.1.
The palms of the driver test dummy shall be in contact with the outer part of
the steering wheel rim at the rim's horizontal centreline. The thumbs shall be
over the steering wheel rim and shall be lightly taped to the steering wheel rim
so that if the hand of the test dummy is pushed upward by a force of not less
than 9 N and not more than 22 N, the tape shall release the hand from the
steering wheel rim.
