Fire resistance
This test is required for REESS containing flammable electrolyte.

mounted such that the lowest surface of the casing of the REESS is more than
1.5m above the ground. At the option of the manufacturer, this test may be
performed where the of the REESS’s lower surface is higher than 1.5 m
