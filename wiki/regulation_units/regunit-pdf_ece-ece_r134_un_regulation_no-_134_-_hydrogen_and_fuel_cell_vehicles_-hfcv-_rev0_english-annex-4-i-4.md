## Basis
(i)
Sulphuric acid -19 per cent solution by volume in water;
(ii)
Sodium hydroxide -25 per cent solution by weight in water;
(iii)
Ammonium nitrate -28 per cent by weight in water; and
(iv)
Windshield washer fluid (50 per cent by volume methyl
alcohol and water).
The fluids are replenished as needed to ensure complete exposure for
the duration of the test. A distinct test is performed with each of the
fluids. One component may be used for exposure to all of the fluids in
sequence.
