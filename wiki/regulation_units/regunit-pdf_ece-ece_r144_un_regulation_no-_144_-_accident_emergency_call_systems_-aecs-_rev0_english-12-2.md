## Basis
12.
Names and addresses of Technical Services
responsible for conducting approval tests and of
Type Approval Authorities
The Contracting Parties to the Agreement applying this Regulation shall
communicate to the United Nations Secretariat the names and addresses of
the Technical Services responsible for conducting approval tests and of the
Type Approval Authorities which grant approval and to which forms
certifying approval or refusal, or extension or withdrawal of approval, issued
in other countries, are to be sent.
E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.3/Add.143
Part Ib - Approval of AECD which are intended to be fitted
to vehicles of categories M1 and N1
