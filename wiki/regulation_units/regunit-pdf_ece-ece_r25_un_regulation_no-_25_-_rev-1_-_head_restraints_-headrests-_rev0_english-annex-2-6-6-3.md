## Basis
6.6.3.
In the case of head restraints adjustable for height, one or more
gaps, which regardless of its shape can show a distance 'a'
measured as described in paragraph 7.5 of more than 60 mm, are
permitted on the part of the device serving as the head restraint
provided that, after the additional test under paragraph 7.4.3.4,
the requirements of paragraph 7.4.3.6 are still met.
