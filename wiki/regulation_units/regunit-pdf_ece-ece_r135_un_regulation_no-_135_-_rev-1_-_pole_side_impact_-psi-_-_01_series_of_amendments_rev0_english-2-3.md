## Basis
2.3.
"Compressed hydrogen storage system (CHSS)" means a system designed to
store hydrogen fuel for a hydrogen-fuelled vehicle and composed of a
pressurized container, pressure relief devices (PRDs) and shut off device that
isolate the stored hydrogen from the remainder of the fuel system and the
environment.
