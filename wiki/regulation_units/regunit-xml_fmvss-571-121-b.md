# (b) An emergency system that automatically applies the service brakes when the service reservoir is at any pressure abov

## Statement
(b) An emergency system that automatically applies the service brakes when the service reservoir is at any pressure above 20 lb/in 2 and the supply line is at atmospheric pressure. However, any agricultural commodity trailer, heavy hauler trailer, or pulpwood trailer shall meet the requirements of S5.8.1 or, at the option of the manufacturer, the requirements of § 393.43 of this title.
