## Basis
3.3.3.3.
Reversing test in standstill condition
A vehicle tested in an indoor or outdoor facility, shall be located with the rear
plane of the vehicle on the PP' line as shown in Figure 2b of the Appendix to
this annex.
The vehicle's gear selection control shall be in the reverse position and the
brake released for the test.
