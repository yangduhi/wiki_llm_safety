## Basis
20.2.
If a Contracting Party to the Agreement which applies this Regulation
withdraws an approval it has previously granted, it shall forthwith so notify
the other Contracting Parties applying this Regulation by means of a copy of
the approval form bearing at the end, in large letters, the signed and dated
annotation "APPROVAL WITHDRAWN".
