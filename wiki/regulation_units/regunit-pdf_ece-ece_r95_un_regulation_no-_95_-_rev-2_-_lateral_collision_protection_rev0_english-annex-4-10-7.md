## Basis
10.7.
As from 24 months after the official date of entry into force of the 03 series
of amendments, Contracting Parties applying this Regulation shall grant
approvals only to those types of vehicles which comply with the
requirements of this Regulation as amended by the 03 series of amendments.
However, in the case of vehicles having an electrical power train operating on
high voltage, an additional period of 12 months is granted provided that the
manufacturer demonstrates, to the satisfaction of the Technical Service, that
the vehicle provides equivalent levels of safety to those required by this
Regulation as amended by the 03 series of amendments.
