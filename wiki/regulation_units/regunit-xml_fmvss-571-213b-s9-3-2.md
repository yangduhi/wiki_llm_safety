# S9.3.2 When using the test dummies conforming to subparts N, P, R, S or T, prepare the dummies as specified in this para

## Statement
S9.3.2 When using the test dummies conforming to subparts N, P, R, S or T, prepare the dummies as specified in this paragraph. Before being used in testing under this standard, dummies must be conditioned at any ambient temperature from 20.6° to 22.2 °C and at any relative humidity from 10 percent to 70 percent, for at least 4 hours.
