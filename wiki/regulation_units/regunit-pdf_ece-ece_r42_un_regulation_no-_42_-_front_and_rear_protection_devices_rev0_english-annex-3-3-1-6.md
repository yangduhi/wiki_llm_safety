## Basis
3.1.6.
"Reference height" means the height above the ground at which the
vehicle should embody sufficient protective devices, both at
“unladen kerb weight" and at "laden test weight". This reference
height is that of the horizontal plane passing through the reference
line of the impactor described in paragraph 2.3. of annex 3 to this
Regulation;
