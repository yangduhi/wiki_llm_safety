## Basis
17.3.
Position determination
If the AECD is fitted, in accordance with paragraph 1.4., with a GNSS
receiver supporting at least three GNSS including GLONASS, GALILEO
and GPS, and is able to receive and process SBAS signals, then the AECD
shall comply with the requirements of paragraphs 17.3.1. to 17.3.11.
AECD compliance with respect to positioning capabilities shall be
demonstrated by performing the test methods described in Annex 10: Test
E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.3/Add.143
methods for the navigation solutions. It shall be indicated in the
communication document of Annex 2, item 11.
