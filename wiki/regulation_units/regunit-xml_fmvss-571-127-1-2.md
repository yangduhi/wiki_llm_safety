# (1) The visual signal symbol must be located within an ellipse that extends ±18 degrees vertically and ±10 degrees horiz

## Statement
(1) The visual signal symbol must be located within an ellipse that extends ±18 degrees vertically and ±10 degrees horizontally of the driver forward line of sight based on the forward-looking eye midpoint (M f ) as described in S14 of 49 CFR 571.111.
