# S8.7 Small volume manufacturers. Vehicles manufactured during any of the three years of the September 1, 2012 through Au

## Statement
S8.7 Small volume manufacturers. Vehicles manufactured during any of the three years of the September 1, 2012 through August 31, 2015 phase-in by a manufacturer that produces fewer than 5,000 vehicles for sale in the United States during that year are not subject to the requirements of S8.1, S8.2, and S8.3.
