## Basis
11.
Names and addresses of Technical Services
responsible for conducting approval tests and of
Type Approval Authorities
The Parties to the Agreement which apply this Regulation shall communicate
to the Secretariat of the United Nations the names and addresses of the
Technical Services conducting approval tests, and of the Type Approval
Authorities which grant approval and to which forms certifying approval or
extension, or refusal or withdrawal of approval, issued in other countries, are
to be sent.
E/ECE/324/Rev.1/Add.28/Rev.2
