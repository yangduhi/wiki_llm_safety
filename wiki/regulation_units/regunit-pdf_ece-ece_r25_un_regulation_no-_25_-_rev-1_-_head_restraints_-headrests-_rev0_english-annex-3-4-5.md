## Basis
4.5.
Place the seat and back assembly of the 3/DH machine so that the
centreplane of the occupant (C/LO) coincides with the centreplane of
the 3/DH machine. At the manufacturer's request, the 3/DH machine
may be moved inboard with respect to the C/LO if the 3/DH machine is
located so far outboard that the seat edge will not permit levelling
of the 3/DH machine.
