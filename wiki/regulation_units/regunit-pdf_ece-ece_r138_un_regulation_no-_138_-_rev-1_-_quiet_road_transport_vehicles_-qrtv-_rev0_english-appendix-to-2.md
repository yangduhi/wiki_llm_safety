## Basis
Appendix to this annex on the test track or in an indoor test facility shall be
2,0 m ± 0,05 m.
The microphones shall be located 1,2 m ± 0,02 m above the ground level.
The reference direction for free field conditions as specified in IEC 61672-
1:2013 shall be horizontal and directed perpendicularly towards the path of
the vehicle line CC'.
