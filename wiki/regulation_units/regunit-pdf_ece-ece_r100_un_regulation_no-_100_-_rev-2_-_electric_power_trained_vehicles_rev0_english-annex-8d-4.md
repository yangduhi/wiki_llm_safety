## Basis
Annex 8D
A higher crush force, a longer onset time, a longer hold time, or a
combination of these, may be applied at the request of the manufacturer.
The application of the force shall be decided by the manufacturer together
with the Technical Service having consideration to the direction of travel of
the REESS relative to its installation in the vehicle. The application force
being applied horizontally and perpendicular to the direction of travel of the
REESS.
The test shall end with an observation period of 1 h at the ambient
temperature conditions of the test environment.
Dimension of the crush plate:
600 mm x 600 mm or smaller
E/ECE/324/Rev.2/Add.99/Rev.2
E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.2/Add.99/Rev.2
