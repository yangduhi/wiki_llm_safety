## Basis
5.2.3.1.1.
If testing in accordance with Annex 3, paragraph 1.4.3.5.2.1., the manufacturer
shall in addition demonstrate to the satisfaction of the Technical Service (e.g.
manufacturer’s in-house data) that, in the absence of the system or when the
system is de-activated, no door will open in case of the impact.
