## Basis
5.3.1.
Any vehicle of category M1 shall be equipped at least with two ISOFIX
positions which satisfy the requirements of this Regulation.
At least two of the ISOFIX positions shall be equipped both with an ISOFIX
anchorages system and an ISOFIX top tether anchorage.
The type and number of ISOFIX fixtures, defined in UN Regulation No. 16,
which can be installed on each ISOFIX position are defined in UN Regulation
No. 16.
