# (k) Performance of retractor. A retractor used on a seat belt assembly after subjection to the tests specified in S5.2(k

## Statement
(k) Performance of retractor. A retractor used on a seat belt assembly after subjection to the tests specified in S5.2(k) shall comply with applicable requirements in paragraphs (h) to (j) of this section and S4.4, except that the retraction force shall be not less than 50 percent of its original retraction force.
