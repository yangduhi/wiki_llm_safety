## Basis
17.15.
From the date of entry into force of Supplement 4 to the 04 series of
Amendments of this Regulation, by way of derogation to the obligations of
Contracting Parties during the transitional period set out in paragraph 17.14.
and based on the declaration made by the European Community at the time
of its accession to the 1958 Agreement (Depositary Notification
C.N.60.1998.TREATIES-28), member states of the European Community
may prohibit the placing on the market of child restraint systems which do
not meet the requirements of Supplement 4 to the 04 series of Amendments
of this Regulation.
.
