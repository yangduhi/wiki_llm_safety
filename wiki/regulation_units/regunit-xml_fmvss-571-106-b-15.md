# (b) Condition a cylinder in air at a temperature between minus 49 degrees Fahrenheit and minus 54 degrees Fahrenheit (mi

## Statement
(b) Condition a cylinder in air at a temperature between minus 49 degrees Fahrenheit and minus 54 degrees Fahrenheit (minus 45 degrees Celsius and minus 48 degrees Celsius) for 70 hours, using a cylinder of 2 1/2 inches in diameter for tests of hose less than 1/8 inch or 3mm, 3 inches in diameter for tests of 1/8 inch or 3 mm hose, 3 1/2 inches in diameter for tests of 3/16 to 1/4 inch hose or 4 mm to 6 mm hose, and 4 inches in diameter for tests of hose greater than 1/4 inch or 6 mm in diameter.
