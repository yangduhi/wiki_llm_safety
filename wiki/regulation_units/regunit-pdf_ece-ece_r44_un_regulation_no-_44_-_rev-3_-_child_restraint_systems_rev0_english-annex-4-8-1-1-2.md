## Basis
8.1.1.2.
On completion of the exposure test the metal items of the child restraint shall
be gently washed, or dipped, in clean running water with a temperature not
higher than 38 °C to remove any salt deposit that may have formed and then
allowed to dry at room temperature of 18 to 25 °C for 24 ± 1 hours before
inspection in accordance with paragraph 7.1.1.2. above.
