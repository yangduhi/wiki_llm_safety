## Basis
4.4.
The area of the seating position contacted by the 3/DH/machine shall
be covered by a muslin cotton, of sufficient size and appropriate
texture, described as a plain cotton fabric having 18.9 threads
per/cm
2 and weighing 0.228/kg/m
2 or knitted or non-woven fabric
having equivalent characteristics.
If test is run on a seat outside the vehicle, the floor on which the
seat is placed shall have the same essential characteristics 1/ as
the floor of the vehicle in which the seat is intended to be used.
