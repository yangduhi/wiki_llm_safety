# S10.1 Each motorcycle shall have either a mirror of unit magnification with not less than 8065 mm 2 of reflective surfac

## Statement
S10.1 Each motorcycle shall have either a mirror of unit magnification with not less than 8065 mm 2 of reflective surface, or a convex mirror with not less than 6450 mm 2 of reflective surface and an average radius of curvature not less than 508 mm and not greater than 1524 mm, installed with a stable support, and mounted so that the horizontal center of the reflective surface is at least 279 mm outward of the longitudinal centerline of the motorcycle. The mirror shall be adjustable by tilting in both the horizontal and vertical directions.
