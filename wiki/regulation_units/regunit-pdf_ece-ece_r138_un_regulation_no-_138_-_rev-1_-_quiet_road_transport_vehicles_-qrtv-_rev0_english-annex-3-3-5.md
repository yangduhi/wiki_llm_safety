## Basis
3.5.
Data compilation and reported results
For each test condition described in paragraph 3.3., the background corrected
results, Ltestcorr,j, and the corresponding one third octave spectra of both sides
of the vehicle individually shall be arithmetically averaged and rounded to
the first decimal place.
The final A-weighted sound pressure level results Lcrs 10, Lcrs 20 and Lreverse to
be reported are the lower values of the two averages of both sides, rounded to
the nearest integer. The final one third octave spectra to be reported are the
spectra corresponding to the same side as the reported A-weighted sound
pressure level.
