## Basis
5.15.
The strength of the seat-back and of its locking devices is deemed to meet the
requirements set out in paragraph 6.2. below when, after testing in
accordance with paragraph 6.4.3.6. below, no breakage of the seat or
seat-back has occurred; otherwise, it shall be shown that the seat is capable of
meeting the test requirements set out in paragraph 6.2. below.
