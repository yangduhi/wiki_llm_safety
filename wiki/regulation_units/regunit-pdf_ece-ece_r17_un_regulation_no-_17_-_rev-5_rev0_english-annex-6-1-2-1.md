## Basis
1.2.1.
This apparatus consists of a pendulum whose pivot is supported by
ball-bearings and whose reduced mass* at its centre of percussion is 6.8 kg.
The lower extremity of the pendulum consists of a rigid headform 165 mm in
diameter whose centre is identical with the centre of percussion of the
pendulum.
