## Basis
2.12.
"Seat cushion reference line" means a planar line along the side surface of the
seat cushion base and passing through the SCRP defined in paragraph 2.14.
of this annex. The seat cushion reference line may be marked on the side of a
seat cushion support structure and/or its position defined using an additional
reference point. The projection of the seat cushion reference line to a vertical
longitudinal plane is linear (i.e. straight).
