## Basis
4.5.1.10.
The braking performance of the axle under test shall be determined by calculating
the deceleration determined from a direct measurement of velocity and distance
between 0.8 v1 and v2, where v2 shall not be less than 0.1 v1. This shall be
deemed to be equivalent to the mean fully developed deceleration (MFDD) as
defined in Annex 4 above.
