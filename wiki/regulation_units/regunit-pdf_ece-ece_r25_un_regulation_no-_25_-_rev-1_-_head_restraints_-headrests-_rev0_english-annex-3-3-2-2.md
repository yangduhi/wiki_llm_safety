## Basis
3.2.2.
The relative positions of the "R" point and the "H" point and the
relationship between the design torso angle and the actual torso
angle shall be considered satisfactory for the seating position in
question if the "H" point, as defined by its co-ordinates, lies
within a square of 50/mm side length with horizontal and vertical
sides whose diagonals intersect at the "R" point, and if the actual
torso angle is within 5 degree of the design of the torso angle.
