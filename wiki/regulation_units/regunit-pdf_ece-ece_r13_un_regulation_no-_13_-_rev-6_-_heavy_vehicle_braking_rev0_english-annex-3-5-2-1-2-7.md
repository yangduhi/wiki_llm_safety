5.2.1.2.7.1.
If service braking is ensured by the action of the driver's muscular energy assisted
by one or more energy reserves, secondary braking shall, in the event of failure of
that assistance, be capable of being ensured by the driver's muscular energy

force applied to the control not exceeding the prescribed maxima;
5.2.1.2.7.2.
If the service braking force and transmission depend exclusively on the use,
