## Basis
17.11.
During the 12-month period which follows the date of entry into force of
the 04 series of amendments, Contracting Parties applying this Regulation
can continue to grant type approvals to those child restraint systems which
comply with the requirements of this Regulation as amended by the 03 series
of amendments.
