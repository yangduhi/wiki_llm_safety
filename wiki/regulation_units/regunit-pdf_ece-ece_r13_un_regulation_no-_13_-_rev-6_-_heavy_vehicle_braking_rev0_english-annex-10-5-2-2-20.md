## Basis
5.2.2.20.
When the supply voltage to the trailer falls below a value nominated by the
manufacturer at which the prescribed service braking performance can no longer
be
guaranteed,
the
separate
yellow
warning
signal
specified
in
paragraph 5.2.1.29.2. shall be activated via pin 5 of the ISO 7638:1997 17/
connector. In addition, trailers equipped with an electrical control line, when
electrically connected to a towing vehicle with an electric control line, shall
provide the failure information for actuation of the red warning signal specified in
paragraph 5.2.1.29.2.1. via the data communication part of the electric control
line.
