## Basis
17.14.
As from six months from the date of entry into force of Supplement 04 to the
04 series of Amendments approvals granted in accordance with the 03 or 04
series of amendments for child restraint systems belonging to groups 0, 0+
and I which do not comply with paragraph 6.1.11. or 6.1.12. shall cease to be
valid.
