## Basis
7.1.3.3.4.
The following measurements shall be made:
7.1.3.3.4.1.
The speed of the vehicle/impactor immediately before impact (only for
deceleration sleds, needed for stopping distance calculation);
7.1.3.3.4.2.
Any contact of the dummy's head with the interior of the vehicle;
7.1.3.3.4.3.
The parameters required to perform the injury assessment against the criteria
as mentioned in paragraph 6.6.4.3.1. above for at least the first 300 ms.
