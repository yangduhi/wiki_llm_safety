## Basis
3.3.1.
General
For each operating condition, the vehicle can be tested either indoor or
outdoor.
For constant speed and reversing tests the vehicle may be tested either in
motion or in simulated operating condition. For simulated vehicle operation,
signals shall be applied to the vehicle to simulate actual in-use operation.
If the vehicle is equipped with an internal combustion engine, it shall be
turned off.
