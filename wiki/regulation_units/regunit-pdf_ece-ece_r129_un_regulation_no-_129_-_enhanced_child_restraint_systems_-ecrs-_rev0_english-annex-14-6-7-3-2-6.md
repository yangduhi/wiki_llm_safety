## Basis
6.7.3.2.6.
The strap shall be repeatedly withdrawn from the retractor and allowed to
retract, in the conditions prescribed in paragraph 7.2.4.2. below, until
40,000 cycles have been completed. The retractor shall then be subjected to
the temperature test operation requirements given in paragraph 7.2.4.1. and
corrosion test described in paragraph 8.1.1. below and to the dust-resistance
E/ECE/324/Rev.2/Add.128
E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.2/Add.128
test described in paragraph 7.2.4.5. below. It shall then satisfactorily
complete a further 5,000 cycles of withdrawal and retraction (making 45,000
in all). After the above tests the retractor shall continue to operate correctly
and to meet the requirements of paragraphs 6.7.3.2.1. to 6.7.3.2.5. above.
