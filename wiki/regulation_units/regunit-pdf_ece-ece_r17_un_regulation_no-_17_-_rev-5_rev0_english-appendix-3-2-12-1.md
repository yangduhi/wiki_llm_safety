## Basis
2.12.1.
"Integrated head restraint" means a head restraint formed by the upper part
of the seat-back. Head restraints meeting the definitions of paragraphs 2.12.2.
or 2.12.3. below but which can only be detached from the seat or the vehicle
structure by the use of tools or by partial or complete removal of the seat
