## Basis
2.1.2.
Outdoor testing
The test site shall be substantially level. The test track construction and
surface shall meet the requirements of ISO 10844:2014.
Within a radius of 50 m around the centre of the track, the space shall be free
of large reflecting objects such as fences, rocks, bridges or buildings. The test
track and the surface of the site shall be dry and free from absorbing
materials such as powdery snow, or loose debris.
In the vicinity of the microphones, there shall be no obstacle that could
influence the acoustic field and no person shall remain between the
microphone and the noise source. The meter observer shall be positioned so
as not to influence the meter reading. Microphones shall be located as
specified in Figures 1 of the Appendix to this annex.
