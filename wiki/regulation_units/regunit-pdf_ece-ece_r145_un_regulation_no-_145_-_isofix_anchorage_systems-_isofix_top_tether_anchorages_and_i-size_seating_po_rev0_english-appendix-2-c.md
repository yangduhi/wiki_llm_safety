## Basis
(c)
Yaw: 0°  10°.
For i-Size positions, providing the limits specified in paragraph 5.2.2.4. are
not exceeded, it is acceptable for the shortest support-leg length, according to
the support-leg foot assessment volume, to result in a pitch angle greater than
E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.3/Add.144
would otherwise be imposed by the vehicle seat or structure. It shall be
possible to install the ISOFIX child restraint fixture under the increased pitch
angle. This paragraph does not apply to child restraint fixtures of size
ISO/B2.
