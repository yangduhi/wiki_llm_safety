## Basis
5.2.1.12.
In hydraulic-transmission braking systems, the filling ports of the fluid reservoirs
shall be readily accessible; in addition, the receptacles containing the reserve fluid
shall be so designed and constructed that the level of the reserve fluid can be
easily checked without the receptacles having to be opened. If this latter
condition
is
not
fulfilled,
the
red
warning
signal
specified
in
paragraph 5.2.1.29.1.1. shall draw the driver's attention to any fall in the level of
reserve fluid liable to cause a failure of the braking system. The type of fluid to
be used in the hydraulic transmission braking systems shall be identified by the
symbol in accordance with figure 1 or 2 of Standard ISO 9128:1987. The symbol
shall be affixed in a visible position in indelible form within 100 mm of the filling
ports of the fluid reservoirs; additional information may be provided by the
manufacturer.
