## Basis
15.3.9.
A general warning shall be given to the user concerning the danger of making
any alterations or additions to the device without the approval of the Type
E/ECE/324/Rev.1/Add.43/Rev.3
E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.1/Add.43/Rev.3
Approval Authority, and a danger of not following closely the installation
instructions provided by the child restraint manufacturer.
