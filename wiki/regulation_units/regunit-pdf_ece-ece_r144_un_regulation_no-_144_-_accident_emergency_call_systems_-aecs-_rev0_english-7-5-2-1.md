## Basis
7.5.2.1.
The manufacturer shall provide the Type Approval Authority with an
explanation and technical documentation which shows, in overall terms, how
the malfunction indication strategy is achieved. This documentation shall be
maintained by the manufacturer and shall be made open for inspection by the
Technical Service at the time of the type approval (if the AECC is fitted with
corresponding component).
