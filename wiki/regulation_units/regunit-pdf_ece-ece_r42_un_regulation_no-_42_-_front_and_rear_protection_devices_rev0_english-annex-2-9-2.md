## Basis
9.2.
If a Party to the Agreement which applies this Regulation withdraws
an approval it has previously granted, it shall forthwith so notify
the other Contracting Parties to the Agreement which apply this
Regulation, by means of a copy of the approval form bearing at the
end, in large letters, the signed and dated annotation "APPROVAL
WITHDRAWN”.
