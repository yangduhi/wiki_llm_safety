## Basis
5.1.6.
None of the tests A, B, C, need be carried out if the manufacturer can show
by computer simulation or calculations of the strength of the component parts
of the cab or by other means to the satisfaction of the Technical Service that
the cab will not undergo deformation dangerous to the occupants (penetration
into the survival space) if subjected to the conditions of the tests.
