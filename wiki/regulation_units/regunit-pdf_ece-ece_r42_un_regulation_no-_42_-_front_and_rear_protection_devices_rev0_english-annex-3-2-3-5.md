## Basis
2.3.5.
The first contact of the impactor with the vehicle shall be by the
impact contour on the protective device. In the two vehicle-loading
conditions the protective device between the corners, as defined in
paragraph 3.1.5. of this Regulation, shall be intersected by the
horizontal plane passing through the reference line.
