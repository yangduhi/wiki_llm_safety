## Basis
5.5.1.
The collision warning referred to in paragraph 5.2.1.1. above shall be
provided by at least two modes selected from acoustic, haptic or optical.
The timing of the warning signals shall be such that they provide the
possibility for the driver to react to the risk of collision and take control of
the situation, and shall also avoid nuisance for the driver by too early or too
frequent warnings. This shall be tested in accordance with the provisions of
paragraphs 6.4.2. and 6.5.2. of this Regulation
