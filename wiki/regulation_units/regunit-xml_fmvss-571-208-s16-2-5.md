# S16.2.5 The dummy is clothed in form fitting cotton stretch garments with short sleeves and above the knee length pants.

## Statement
S16.2.5 The dummy is clothed in form fitting cotton stretch garments with short sleeves and above the knee length pants. A size 7 1/2 W shoe which meets the configuration and size specifications of MIL-S-21711E (incorporated by reference, see § 571.5) or its equivalent is placed on each foot of the test dummy.
