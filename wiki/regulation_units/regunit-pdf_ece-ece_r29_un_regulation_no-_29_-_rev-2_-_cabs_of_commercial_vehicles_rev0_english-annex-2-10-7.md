## Basis
10.7.
Contracting Parties applying this Regulation shall continue to grant approvals
to those types of vehicles which comply with the requirements of this
Regulation as amended by the preceding series of amendments during
the 72 months’ period which follows the date of entry into force of the 03
series of amendments.
