  attachments (if any), and all closure devices required to isolate the stored hydrogen

  it to high pressure while simultaneously cooling it to very low temperatures, allowing
  for a higher density of hydrogen storage compared to standard compressed hydrogen
  systems.

  combustion engine, fuel lines, and exhaust systems.
