## Basis
1.4.
Lateral attachment
Lateral movement shall be limited by chains or ropes В attached to the
chassis frame symmetrically in relation to its longitudinal axis. The points of
attachment to the chassis shall be not more than 5 m and not less than 3 m
from the front of the vehicle. The chains or ropes shall when tensioned form a
downward angle of not more than 20° with the horizontal and their projection
on a horizontal plane shall form an angle of not less than 25° and not more
than 45° with the longitudinal axis of the vehicle.
