# S9.4.1.1 Forces described in S9.4.1(a), forward direction, shall be applied with an initial force application angle of 1

## Statement
S9.4.1.1 Forces described in S9.4.1(a), forward direction, shall be applied with an initial force application angle of 10 ±5 degrees above the horizontal. Forces described in S9.4.1(b), lateral direction, shall be applied horizontally (0 ±5 degrees).
