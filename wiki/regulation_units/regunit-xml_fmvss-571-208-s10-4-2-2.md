# S10.4.2.2 Pelvic angle. As determined using the pelvic angle gage (GM drawing 78051-532, incorporated by reference in pa

## Statement
S10.4.2.2 Pelvic angle. As determined using the pelvic angle gage (GM drawing 78051-532, incorporated by reference in part 572, subpart E of this chapter) which is inserted into the H-point gaging hole of the dummy, the angle measured from the horizontal on the three inch flat surface of the gage shall be 22 1/2 degrees plus or minus 2 1/2 degrees.
