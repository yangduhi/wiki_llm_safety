# (b) Each factory-installed built-in child restraint system shall be permanently labeled with the information specified i

## Statement
(b) Each factory-installed built-in child restraint system shall be permanently labeled with the information specified in S5.5.5(f) through (j) and S5.5.5(l), so that the information is visible when the restraint is activated for use. The information shall also be included in the vehicle owner's manual.
