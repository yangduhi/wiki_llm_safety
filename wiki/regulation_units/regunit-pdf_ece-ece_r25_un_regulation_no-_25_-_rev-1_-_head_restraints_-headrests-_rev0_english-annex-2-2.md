____________
2/

for the Netherlands, 5 for Sweden, 6 for Belgium, 7 for Hungary, 8 for
Czechoslovakia, 9 for Spain, 10 for Yugoslavia, 11 for the United/Kingdom,

United/Nations to the Contracting Parties to the Agreement.
