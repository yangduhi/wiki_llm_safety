## Basis
17.4.
Starting 24 months after the entry into force of the 02 series of amendments
to this Regulation, Contracting Parties applying this Regulation may refuse
first national registration (first entry into service) of a vehicle which does not
meet the requirements of the 02 series of amendments to this Regulation.
