## Basis
Annex 20 shows a typical device for a buckle strength test. The buckle is
placed on the upper round plate (A) within the relief. All adjacent straps have
a length of at least 250 mm and are arranged hanging down from the upper
plate respective to their position at the buckle. The free strap ends are then
wound round the lower round plate (B) until they come out at the plate's inner
opening. All straps have to be vertical between A and B. The round clamping
plate (C) is then lightly clamped against the lower face of (B), still allowing a
certain strap movement between them. With a small force at the tensile
E/ECE/324/Rev.1/Add.43/Rev.3
E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.1/Add.43/Rev.3
machine the straps are tensioned and pulled between (B) and (C) until all
straps are loaded respective to their arrangement. The buckle shall stay free
from plate (A) or any parts at (A) during this operation and the test itself.
