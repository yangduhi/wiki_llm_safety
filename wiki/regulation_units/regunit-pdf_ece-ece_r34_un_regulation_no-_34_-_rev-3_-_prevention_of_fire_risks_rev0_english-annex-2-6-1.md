## Basis
6.1.
Hydraulic test
The tank shall be subjected to a hydraulic internal pressure test which shall
be carried out on an isolated unit complete with all its accessories. The tank
shall be completely filled with a non-flammable liquid (water, for example).
After all communication with the outside has been cut off, the pressure shall
be gradually increased, through the pipe connection through which fuel is fed
to the engine, to a relative internal pressure equal to double the working
pressure used and in any case to not less than an excess pressure of 30 kPa
(0.3) bar, which shall be maintained for one minute. During this time the tank
shell shall not crack or leak; however, it may be permanently deformed.
E/ECE/324/Rev.1/Add.33/Rev.3
