## Basis
16.2.
An approval number shall be assigned to each type approved. The first two
digits (at present 00) shall indicate the series of amendments incorporating
the most recent major technical amendments made to the Regulation at the
time of issue of the approval. The same Contracting Party shall not assign the
same number to another type of AECD.
