## Basis
5.1.1.2.
From an initial speed of not less than 50 km/h, on a surface with a coefficient of
adhesion of 0.3 or less 6/, the brakes of the laden vehicle shall be fully applied for a
time t, during which time the energy consumed by the indirectly controlled wheels
shall be taken into consideration and all directly controlled wheels shall remain under
control of the anti-lock system.
