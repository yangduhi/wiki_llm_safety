## Basis
5.3.4.
In the case of vehicles equipped with anti-lock systems of categories 1 or 2, when
the right and left wheels of the vehicle are situated on surfaces with differing
coefficients of adhesion (kH and kL), where kH > 0.5 and kH/kL > 2, 11/ the
directly controlled wheels shall not lock when the full force 8/ is suddenly applied
on the control device at a speed of 50 km/h.
