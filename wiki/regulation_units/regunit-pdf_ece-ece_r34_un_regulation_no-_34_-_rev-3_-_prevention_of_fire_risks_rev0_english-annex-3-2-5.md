## Basis
2.5.
Velocity on collision
The velocity on collision shall be between 48.3 km/h and 53.1 km/h.
However, if the test has been carried out at a higher collision velocity and the
vehicle has satisfied the conditions prescribed, the test shall be considered
satisfactory.
