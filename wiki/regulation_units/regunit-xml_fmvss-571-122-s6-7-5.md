S6.7.5 Performance requirements. When the brakes are tested in accordance with the test procedure set out in S6.7.4, the stopping distance S 2 shall be ≤1.67 S 1 −0.67 × 0.1V,
Where: S 1 = corrected stopping distance in meters achieved in the baseline test set out in S6.7.2. S 2 = corrected stopping distance in meters achieved in the hot brake stop set out in S6.7.4. V = specified test speed in km/h.
