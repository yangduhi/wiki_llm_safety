## Basis
1.4.4.1.
The braking performance of the trailer can be calculated either from the braking
rate of the towing vehicle plus the trailer and the measured thrust on the coupling
or, in certain cases, from the braking rate of the towing vehicle plus the trailer
with only the trailer being braked. The engine of the towing vehicle shall be
disconnected during the braking test.
In the case where only the trailer is braked, to take account of the extra mass
being retarded, the performance will be taken to be the mean fully developed
deceleration.
