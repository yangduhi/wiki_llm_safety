## Basis
8.2.9.2.
The ISOFIX attachment opening force shall be applied, using a dynamometer
or similar device in, the normal manner and direction as indicated in the
Manufacturers user manual. The contact end shall be a polished metal
hemisphere with radius 2.5 ± 0.1 mm for a release button or a polished metal
hook with a radius of 25 mm.
