## Basis
2.10.
"Satellite-Based Augmentation System (SBAS)" is a system ensuring the
correction of local errors of GNSS systems due to interferences via a network
of ground-based stations. (e.g. the European Geostationary Navigation
Overlay Service (EGNOS), the Wide Area Augmentation System (WAAS) or
the Quasi-Zenith Satellite System (QZSS)).
E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.3/Add.143
