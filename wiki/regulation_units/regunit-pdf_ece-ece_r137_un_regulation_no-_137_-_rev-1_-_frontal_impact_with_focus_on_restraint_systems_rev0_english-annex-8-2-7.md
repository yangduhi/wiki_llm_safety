## Basis
2.7.
Environmental effects
A regular check shall be made to identify any environmental influence (such
as electric or magnetic flux, cable velocity, etc.). This can be done for instance
by recording the output of spare channels equipped with dummy transducers.
If significant output signals are obtained corrective action shall be taken, for
instance by replacement of cables.
