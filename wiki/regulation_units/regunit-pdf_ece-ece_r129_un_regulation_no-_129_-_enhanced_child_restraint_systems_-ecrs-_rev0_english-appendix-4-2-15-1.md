## Basis
2.15.1.
"Support-leg foot" means one or more part(s) of the support-leg of the Child
Restraint System intended (by design) to engage with the vehicle floor
contact surface and designed to transmit the loading from the support-leg to
the vehicle structure during a frontal impact.
