## Basis
2.2.
Test procedure for the assessment of panel padding material
The test set up consists of a simple drop test using a spherical head form. The
spherical head form has a diameter of 150 mm and a mass of 6 kg (±0.1 kg).
The impact speed is 4 m/s (±0.1 m/s). The instrumentation should allow the
assessment of the time of first contact between the impactor and the sample
as well as the head form acceleration at least in direction of impact
(Z-direction).
The material sample should have the dimensions of 400 x 400 mm. The
sample should be impacted in its centre.
