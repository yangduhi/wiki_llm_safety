## Basis
3.2.2.
Sensors are calibrated to traceable references to ensure an accuracy of
±5 per cent at the targeted criteria of 4 per cent hydrogen or 3 per cent helium
by volume in air, and a full scale measurement capability of at least 25 per
cent above the target criteria. The sensor shall be capable of a 90 per cent
response to a full scale change in concentration within 10 seconds.
