## Basis
2.12.
"ISOFIX attachment" means one of the two connections, fulfilling the
requirements of UN Regulation No. 44 or UN Regulation No. 129, extending
from the ISOFIX child restraint system structure and compatible with an
ISOFIX low anchorage.
