## Basis
3.2.2.4.
Supply line braking request:
For power-driven vehicles which can be operated with trailers connected via an
electric control line only:
Only the electric control line shall be connected.
Simulate message EBS 22, byte 4 with bits 3 - 4 set to 01b and check that when
the service brake, secondary brake or parking brake is fully actuated the pressure
in the supply line falls to 150 kPa within the following two seconds.
Simulate a continuous absence of data communication and check that when the
service brake, secondary brake or parking brake is fully actuated the pressure in
the supply line falls to 150 kPa within the following two seconds.
2/
Optional on towing vehicles with electric and pneumatic control lines when the pneumatic
control line fulfils the relevant requirements for secondary braking.
