## Basis
3.7.4.
Phase D: End of test (Figure 4)
The burning pan covered with the screen shall be moved back to the position
described in phase A. No extinguishing of the tested-device shall be done.
After removal of the pan the tested-device shall be observed until such time
as the surface temperature of the tested-device has decreased to ambient
temperature or has been decreasing for a minimum of 3 hours.
