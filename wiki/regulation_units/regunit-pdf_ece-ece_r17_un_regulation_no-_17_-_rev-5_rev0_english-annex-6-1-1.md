## Basis
1.1.
Setting up
The seat, as mounted in the vehicle, shall be firmly secured to the test bench
with the attachment parts provided by the manufacturer, so as to remain
stationary when the impact is applied.
The seat-back, if adjustable, shall be locked in the position specified in
paragraph 6.1.1. of this Regulation.
If the seat is fitted with a head restraint, the head restraint shall be mounted
on the seat-back as in the vehicle. Where the head restraint is separate, it shall
be secured to the part of the vehicle structure to which it is normally attached.
If the head restraint is adjustable, it shall be placed in the most unfavourable
position allowed by its adjusting systems.
