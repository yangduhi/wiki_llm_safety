2.2.1.4.
the maximum design speed;
1/
As defined in Annex 7 to the Consolidated Resolution on the Construction of Vehicles
(R.E.3) (TRANS/WP.29/78/Rev.1/Amend.2, as last amended by Amend.4).
2/
In accordance with the application dates contained in paragraph 12. of this Regulation,
braking requirements for vehicles of category M1 are exclusively included in Regulation
No. 13-H. For vehicles of category N1, Contracting Parties that are signatories to both
Regulation No. 13-H and this Regulation shall recognize approvals to either Regulation as
equally valid.
