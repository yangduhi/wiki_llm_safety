## Basis
2.15.
"Mass of a vehicle in running order" means the mass of an unladen vehicle
with bodywork, including coolant, oils, 90 per cent of fuel, 100 per cent of
other liquids, driver (75 kg) but except used waters, tools, spare wheel.
