## Basis
2.11.
For other seating positions, without inducing pelvis or torso movement,
place the heels of the dummy as far forward as possible on the floor-pan
without compressing the seat cushion more than the compression due to the
weight of the leg. Set the knees of the dummy such that their outside surfaces
are 150 ± 10 mm from the plane of symmetry of the dummy.
E/ECE/324/Rev.1/Add.94/Rev.2
