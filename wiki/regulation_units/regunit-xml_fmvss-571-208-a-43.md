# (a) Definitions for S7.5. (1) A manual seat belt is not in use when the seat belt latch mechanism is not fastened. A sea

## Statement
(a) Definitions for S7.5. (1) A manual seat belt is not in use when the seat belt latch mechanism is not fastened. A seat belt is in use when the seat belt latch mechanism is fastened. An automatic seat belt is not in use when the seat belt latch mechanism is not fastened or, if the automatic belt is non-detachable, the emergency release mechanism is in the released position. If the automatic seat belt is motorized, whether the seat belt is in use is determined when the seat belt webbing is in its locked protective mode at the anchorage point.
