## Basis
4.3.
For each type of AECC, the application shall be accompanied by samples of
complete sets of AECCs, representative of the type to be approved, in
sufficient quantities for the tests prescribed by this Regulation. Additional
specimens may be called for at the request of the Technical Service
responsible for conducting the test.
