# S7. Ejection mitigation test device specifications. The ejection mitigation test device consists of an ejection impactor

## Statement
S7. Ejection mitigation test device specifications. The ejection mitigation test device consists of an ejection impactor and ejection propulsion mechanism with the following specifications. The ability of a test device to meet these specifications may be determined outside of the vehicle.
