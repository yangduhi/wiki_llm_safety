# Annex 4A or Annex 4B of this Regulation or the protection degree IPXXB

## Basis
Annex 4A or Annex 4B of this Regulation or the protection degree IPXXB
shall be fulfilled for the Tested-Device.
If tested according to paragraph 6.4.2.2., the evidence of electrolyte leakage
shall be verified by visual inspection without disassembling any part of the
tested-device.
To confirm compliance to (c1) of paragraph 6.4.2.3. an appropriate coating
shall, if necessary, be applied to the physical protection (casing) in order to
confirm if there is any electrolyte leakage from the REESS resulting from the
impact test. Unless the manufacturer provides a means to differentiate
between the leakage of different liquids, all liquid leakage shall be considered
as the electrolyte.
