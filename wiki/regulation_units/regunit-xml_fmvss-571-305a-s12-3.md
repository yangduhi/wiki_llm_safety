# S12.3 Overcurrent test. The overcurrent test is only conducted on vehicles that have the capability of charging by DC ex

## Statement
S12.3 Overcurrent test. The overcurrent test is only conducted on vehicles that have the capability of charging by DC external electricity supply. The test is conducted at ambient temperatures between 10 °C and 30 °C, with the vehicle REESS initially set between 40 to 50 percent SOC.
