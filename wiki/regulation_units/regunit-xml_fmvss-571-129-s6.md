# S6. Nonconforming tires. Any non-pneumatic tire that is designed for use on passenger cars that does not conform to all 

## Statement
S6. Nonconforming tires. Any non-pneumatic tire that is designed for use on passenger cars that does not conform to all the requirements of this standard, shall not be sold, offered for sale, introduced or delivered for introduction into interstate commerce, or imported into the United States, for any purpose.
