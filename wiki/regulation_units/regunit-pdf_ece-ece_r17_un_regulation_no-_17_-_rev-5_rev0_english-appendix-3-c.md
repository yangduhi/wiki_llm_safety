## Basis
(c)
Vehicles of category M1 with regard to the design of the rear parts of
seat backs and the design of devices intended to protect the occupants
from the danger resulting from the displacement of luggage in a
frontal impact.
It does not apply to vehicles with regard to side-facing or rearward-facing
seats, or to any head restraint fitted to these seats.
