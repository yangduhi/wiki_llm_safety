# S5.1.6. Labeling. Each vehicle container shall be permanently labeled with the information specified in paragraphs S5.1.

## Statement
S5.1.6. Labeling. Each vehicle container shall be permanently labeled with the information specified in paragraphs S5.1.6(a) through (g). Any label affixed to the container in compliance with this section shall remain in place and be legible for the vehicle manufacturer's recommended service life of the container. The information shall be in English and in letters and numbers that are at least 6.35 millimeters ( 1/4 inch) high.
