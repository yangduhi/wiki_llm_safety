## Basis
2.
For rearward-facing devices there shall be side wings with a depth of
minimum 90 mm measured from the median of the surface of the backrest.
These side wings shall start at the horizontal plane passing through point "A"
and continue to the top of the seat back. Starting from a point 90 mm below
the top of the seat back, the depth of the side wing may be gradually reduced.
