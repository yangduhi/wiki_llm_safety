# (i) Strap. A strap used in a seat belt assembly to sustain restraint forces shall comply with the requirements for webbi

## Statement
(i) Strap. A strap used in a seat belt assembly to sustain restraint forces shall comply with the requirements for webbing in S4.2, and if the strap is made from a rigid material, it shall comply with applicable requirements in S4.2, S4.3, and S4.4.
