## Basis
7.2.5.2.4.
Heat conditioning
7.2.5.2.4.1.
The strap shall be kept for 180 ± 10 minutes in a heating-cabinet atmosphere
having a temperature of 60 °C ± 5 °C and a relative humidity 65 ± 5 per cent.
E/ECE/324/Rev.2/Add.128
E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.2/Add.128
7.2.5.2.4.2.
The breaking load shall be determined within five minutes after removal of
the strap from the heating cabinet.
