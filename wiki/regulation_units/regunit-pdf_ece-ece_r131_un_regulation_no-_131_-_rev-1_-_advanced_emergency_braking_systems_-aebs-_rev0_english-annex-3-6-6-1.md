## Basis
6.6.1.
Simulate an electrical failure, for example by disconnecting the power source
to any AEBS component or disconnecting any electrical connection between
AEBS components. When simulating an AEBS failure, neither the electrical
connections for the driver warning signal of paragraph 5.5.4. above nor the
optional manual AEBS deactivation control of paragraph 5.4. shall be
disconnected.
