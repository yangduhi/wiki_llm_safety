## Basis
6.1.1.
Child Restraint Systems in the i-Size category are for use in i-Size seating
positions, when the Child Restraint Systems are fitted in conformity with the
vehicle manufacturer's instructions.
Child Restraint Systems in the "Specific vehicle ISOFIX" category are for
use in all ISOFIX positions and also in the luggage area, if the restraints are
fitted in conformity with the vehicle manufacturer's instructions.
