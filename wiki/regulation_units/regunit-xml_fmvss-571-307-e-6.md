# (e) The measurement device shall have a measurement response time (t 0 −t 90 ) of less than 2 seconds, where t 0 is the 

## Statement
(e) The measurement device shall have a measurement response time (t 0 −t 90 ) of less than 2 seconds, where t 0 is the moment of hydrogen concentration switching, and t 90 is the time when 90 percent of the final indication is reached and shall have a resolution time of less than 300 milliseconds (sampling rate of greater than 3.33 Hz).
