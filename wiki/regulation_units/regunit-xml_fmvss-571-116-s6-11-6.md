# S6.11.6 Calculation. Determine corrosion loss by dividing the change in mass of each metal strip by the total surface ar

## Statement
S6.11.6 Calculation. Determine corrosion loss by dividing the change in mass of each metal strip by the total surface area of each strip measured in square millimeters (mm 2 ), to the nearest square millimeter (mm 2 ). Average the results for the two strips of each type of metal, rounding to the nearest 0.05 mg. per 100 square millimeter (mm 2 ). If only one of the duplicates fails for any reason, run a second set of duplicate samples. Both repeat samples shall meet all requirements of S5.1.11.
