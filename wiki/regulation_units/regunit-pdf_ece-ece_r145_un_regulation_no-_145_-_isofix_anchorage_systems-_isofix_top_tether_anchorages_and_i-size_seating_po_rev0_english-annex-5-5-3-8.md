## Basis
5.3.8.
If a vehicle is only equipped with one seat position per row, only one ISOFIX
position is required in the passenger position. In case where an ISOFIX top
tether anchorage is provided on such vehicles, it shall comply with the suitable
provisions of this Regulation. However where it is not possible to install even
the smallest forward-facing ISOFIX fixture (as defined in UN Regulation
No. 16, Appendix 2, of Annex 17) in the passenger seating position, then no
ISOFIX position shall be required, provided that a child restraint system is
specified for that vehicle.
