## Basis
3.1.7.2.
Six holes are drilled in the bottom-plate in order to fasten it to the trolley with
bolts. The holes are drilled alongside the longest side of the plate, three on
each side, their position being dependent on the construction of the trolley.
Six bolts are put through the holes. It is recommended to glue the bolts on the
plate with an appropriate adhesive. Afterwards, the bolts are fastened with
nuts.
