## Basis
7.2.4.2.
Requirements on installation of the hydrogen storage system not subject to
the lateral impact test:
The container shall be mounted in a position which is between the two
vertical planes parallel to the centre line of the vehicle located 200 mm inside
from the both outermost edge of the vehicle in the proximity of its
container(s).
