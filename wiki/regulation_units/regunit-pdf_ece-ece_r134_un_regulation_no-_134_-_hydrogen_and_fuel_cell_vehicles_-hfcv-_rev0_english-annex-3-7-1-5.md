## Basis
7.1.5.
Fuel system leakage
The hydrogen fuelling line (e.g. piping, joint, etc.) downstream of the main
shut-off valve(s) to the fuel cell system or the engine shall not leak.
Compliance shall be verified at NWP (Annex 5, paragraph 5. test procedure).
