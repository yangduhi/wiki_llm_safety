## Basis
2.15.4.
"Support-leg dimension assessment volume" means a volume defining the
maximum dimensions of a support-leg, corresponding to the support-leg
installation assessment volume for vehicles, as defined in Annex 17 of
Regulation No. 16, ensuring the dimensional installation of a support-leg of
an i-Size CRS in an i-Size seating position of a vehicle.
