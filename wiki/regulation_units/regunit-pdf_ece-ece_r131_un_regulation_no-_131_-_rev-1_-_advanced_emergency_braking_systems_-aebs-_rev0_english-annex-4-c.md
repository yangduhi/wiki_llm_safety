## Basis
(c)
Removal of the high level function.
In case of a failure, the driver shall be warned for example by warning signal
or message display. When the system is not deactivated by the driver, e.g. by
turning the Ignition (run) switch to "off", or by switching off that particular
function if a special switch is provided for that purpose, the warning shall be
present as long as the fault condition persists.
