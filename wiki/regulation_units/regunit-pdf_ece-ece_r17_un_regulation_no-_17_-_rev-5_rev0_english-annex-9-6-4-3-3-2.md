## Basis
6.4.3.3.2.
In the cases described in paragraphs 5.9. and 5.10. above, the test shall be
repeated by applying to each gap, using a sphere of 165 mm in diameter, a
force:
Passing through the centre of gravity of the smallest of the sections of the
gap, along transversal planes parallel to the reference line, and
Reproducing a moment of 37.3 daNm about the R point.
