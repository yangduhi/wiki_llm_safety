## Basis
4.5.2.9.
The brake performance shall be determined by applying the following formula to
the measured brake output torque
where:
Mt
=
Average brake output torque (Nm) - based on distance
g
=
deceleration due to gravity (m/s2)
The average brake output torque (Mt) shall be calculated from the deceleration
determined from a direct measurement of velocity and distance between 0.8 v1
and 0.1 v1. This shall be deemed to be equivalent to the mean fully developed
deceleration (MFDD) as defined in Annex 4 above.
