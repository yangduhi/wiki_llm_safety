## Basis
2.15.
"Support-leg" means an anti–rotation device permanently attached to a Child
Restraint System creating a load path between the Child Restraint System
and the vehicle structure. A support-leg shall be adjustable in length
(Z direction) and may be additionally adjustable in other directions.
