The vehicle shall be laden, the distribution of its mass among the axles being that
stated by the manufacturer; where provision is made for several arrangements of
the load on the axles the distribution of the maximum mass among the axles shall
be such that the load on each axle is proportional to the maximum permissible
2/
The manufacturer shall provide the Technical Service with the family of braking curves
permitted by the automatic control strategy employed. These curves may be verified by the
Technical Service.
