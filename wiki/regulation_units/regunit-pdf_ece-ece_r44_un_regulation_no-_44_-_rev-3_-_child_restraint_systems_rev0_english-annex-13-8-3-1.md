## Basis
8.3.1.
The test seat cushion shall be certified when new to establish initial values
for impact penetration and peak deceleration, and then after every 50
E/ECE/324/Rev.1/Add.43/Rev.3
E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.1/Add.43/Rev.3
dynamic tests or at least every month, whichever is the sooner, or before each
test if the test rig is used frequently.
