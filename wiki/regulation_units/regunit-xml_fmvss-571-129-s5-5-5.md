# S5.5.5 Immediately after running the tire for the required time, allow the tire to cool for one hour, then, if applicabl

## Statement
S5.5.5 Immediately after running the tire for the required time, allow the tire to cool for one hour, then, if applicable, detach it from the non-pneumatic test rim or test wheel center member, and inspect it for the conditions specified in S4.2.2.6.
