## Basis
6.1.
The impactor shall be rigid and its mass shall be evenly-distributed; its mass
shall not be less than 1,000 kg. The impactor shall be cylindrical with a
diameter d of the cylinder of 600 ± 50 mm and a length b of not less than
2,500 mm. Its edges shall be rounded to a radius of curvature of not less than
1.5 mm.
