# (d) Beginning when the headway corresponds to L 0 , the subject vehicle heading is maintained with minimal steering inpu

## Statement
(d) Beginning when the headway corresponds to L 0 , the subject vehicle heading is maintained with minimal steering inputs such that the travel path does not deviate more than 0.3 m laterally from the intended travel path, and the yaw rate of the subject vehicle does not exceed ±1.0 deg/s prior to any automated braking onset.
