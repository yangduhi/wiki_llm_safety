## Basis
1.2.6.
In the case of a vehicle equipped with an anti-lock system of category 3, the value
of zAL will be measured on each axle which has at least one directly controlled
wheel. For example, for a two-axle rear-wheel drive vehicle with an anti-lock
system acting only on the rear axle (2), the adhesion utilized (ε) is given by:
This calculation shall be made for each axle having at least one directly controlled
wheel.
