## Basis
5.5.2.
Electrolyte spillage
In the period from the impact until 30 minutes after, no electrolyte from the
RESS shall spill into the passenger compartment, and no more than 7 per cent
of electrolyte shall spill from the RESS except open type traction batteries
outside the passenger compartment. For open type traction batteries no more
than 7 per cent with a maximum of 5.0 liters shall spill outside the passenger
compartment. The manufacturer shall demonstrate compliance in accordance
with paragraph 6. of Annex 7.
