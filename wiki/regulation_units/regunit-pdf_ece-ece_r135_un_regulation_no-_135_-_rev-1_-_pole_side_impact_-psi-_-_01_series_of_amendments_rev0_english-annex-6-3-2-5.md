## Basis
3.2.5.
The test may be conducted either outdoors in an area protected from the wind
and possible solar effects or indoors in a space that is large enough or
ventilated to prevent the build-up of hydrogen to more than 10 per cent of the
targeted criteria in the passenger and luggage compartments.
