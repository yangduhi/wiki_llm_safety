## Basis
6.8.1.1.3.
In the case of seats or bench seats with head restraints, this area shall extend
between the longitudinal vertical planes, on either side of and 70 mm from
E/ECE/324/Rev.1/Add.16/Rev.5
E/ECE/TRANS/505/Rev.1/Add.16/Rev.5
the longitudinal median plane of the seat or of the seating position concerned
and situated above the plane perpendicular to the reference line 635 mm from
the R point. For the test, the head restraint, if adjustable shall be placed in the
most unfavourable position (generally the highest) permitted by its
adjustment system.
