## Basis
2.2.
Barrier
The barrier consists of a block of reinforced concrete not less than 3 m wide
in front and not less than 1.5 m high. The barrier shall be of such thickness
that it weighs at least 70 tonnes. The front face shall be vertical,
perpendicular to the axis of the run-up track, and covered with plywood
boards 2 cm thick in good condition. The barrier shall be either anchored in
the ground or placed on the ground with, if necessary, additional arresting
devices to limit its displacement. A barrier with different characteristics, but
giving results at least equally conclusive, may likewise be used.
