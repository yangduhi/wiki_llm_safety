## Basis
2.16.
"CRF pitch angle" is the angle between the bottom surface of the fixture
"ISO/F2 (B) as defined in Regulation No. 16 (Annex 17, Appendix 2,
Figure 2) and the horizontal Z plane of the vehicle as defined in Regulation
No. 14 (Annex 4, Appendix 2), with the fixture installed in the vehicle as
defined in Regulation No. 16 (Annex 17, Appendix 2).
