the vehicle user's handbook provided by the manufacturer should warn the driver of

4/
The ISO 7638:1997 connector may be used for 5 pin or 7 pin applications, as appropriate.
5/
It is understood that devices changing the control mode of the anti-lock system are not
subject to paragraph 4.5. of this annex if in the changed control mode condition, all requirements
for the category of anti-lock systems, with which the vehicle is equipped, are fulfilled. However,
in this case, paragraphs 4.5.2., 4.5.3. and 4.5.4. of this annex shall be met.
