# (a) Width. The width of the webbing in a seat belt assembly shall be not less than 46 mm, except for portions that do no

## Statement
(a) Width. The width of the webbing in a seat belt assembly shall be not less than 46 mm, except for portions that do not touch a 95th percentile adult male with the seat in any adjustment position and the seat back in the manufacturer's nominal design riding position when measured under the conditions prescribed in S5.1(a).
