## Basis
3.2.
Discharging
At the beginning of the test, all relevant main contactors shall be closed.
A discharge shall be performed with at least 1/3 C rate but shall not exceed
the maximum current within the normal operating range as specified by the
manufacturer.
The discharging shall be continued until the tested-device (automatically)
interrupts or limits the discharging. Where an automatic interrupt function
fails to operate, or if there is no such function then the discharging shall be
continued until the tested-device is discharged to 25 per cent of its nominal
voltage level.
