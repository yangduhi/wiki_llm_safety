## Basis
1.5.
Tensioning of chains or ropes and rear attachment
The chain or rope С shall, to begin with, be placed under a load of
approximately 1 kN. All slack in the four chains or ropes A and В shall then
be taken up and chain or rope С shall be subjected to a tensile stress of not
less than 10 kN. The angle of chain or rope С with the horizontal shall not
exceed 15°. A vertical blocking force of not less than 500 N shall be applied
at point D between the chassis frame and the ground.
