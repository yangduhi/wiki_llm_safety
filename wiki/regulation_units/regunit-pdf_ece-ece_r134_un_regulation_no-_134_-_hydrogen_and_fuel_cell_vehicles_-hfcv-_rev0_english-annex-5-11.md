## Basis
11.
Production definitively discontinued
If the holder of the approval completely ceases to manufacture a type of
vehicle, system or component approved in accordance with this Regulation,
he shall so inform the authority which granted the approval, which in turn
shall forthwith inform the other Contracting Parties to the Agreement
applying this Regulation by means of a communication form conforming to
the model set out in Part 2 of Annex 1 to this Regulation.
