## Basis
3.2.2.4.
The hydrogen concentration is measured continuously until the concentration
does not rise for 3 minutes. When testing for compliance with paragraph
7.1.4.3 of this Regulation, the simulated leak is then increased using the
remote controllable function until the main hydrogen shut-off valve is closed
and the tell-tale warning signal is activated. The monitoring of the electric
power to the shut-off valve or of the sound of the shut-off valve activation
may be used to confirm the operation of the main shut-off valve of the
hydrogen supply.
