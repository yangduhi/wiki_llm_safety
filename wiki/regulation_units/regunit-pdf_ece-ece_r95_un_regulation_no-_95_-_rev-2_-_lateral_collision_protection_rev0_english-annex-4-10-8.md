## Basis
10.8.
Contracting Parties applying this Regulation shall not refuse to grant
extensions of approvals issued to the preceding series of amendments to this
Regulation, when this extension does not entail any change to the propulsion
system of the vehicle.
However, as from 48 months after the official date of entry into force of the
03 series of amendments, extensions to approvals issued to the previous
series of amendments shall not be granted after this date in respect of vehicles
having an electrical power train operating on high voltage.
