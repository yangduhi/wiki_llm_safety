approved in the Netherlands (E 4) pursuant to Part I of Regulation No. 34 under approval
No. 031234. The first two digits (03) of the approval number indicate that the approval was
granted in accordance with the requirements of Regulation No. 34 as amended by the 03
series of amendments.
