# S7.2 Tires manufactured on or after September 1, 2006 and before September 1, 2007. For tires manufactured on or after S

## Statement
S7.2 Tires manufactured on or after September 1, 2006 and before September 1, 2007. For tires manufactured on or after September 1, 2006 and before September 1, 2007, the number of tires complying with S4.3 of this standard must be equal to not less than 70% of the manufacturer's production during that period.
