## Basis
6.3.1.
A longitudinal horizontal deceleration or, at the choice of the applicant,
acceleration of not less than 20 g shall be applied for 30 milliseconds in a
direction to the whole shell of the vehicle imitating a frontal collision, in
accordance with the requirements of Annex 7, paragraph 1. At the request of
the manufacturer the test pulse described in Annex 9 - Appendix may be used
alternatively.
