## Basis
7.4.3.4.
By means of a spherical head 165/mm in diameter an initial force
producing a moment of 37.3 daNm about the H point shall be applied
at right angles to the displaced reference line R1 at a distance of
65/mm below the top of the head restraint the reference line being
retained in its displaced position R1 as determined in accordance
with the requirements of paragraph/7.4.3.3 above.
