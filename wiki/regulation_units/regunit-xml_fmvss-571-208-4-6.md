# (4) Duty cycle means the total amount of time an intermittent audible warning is active during a warning cycle at the ch

## Statement
(4) Duty cycle means the total amount of time an intermittent audible warning is active during a warning cycle at the chime frequency or continuously, divided by the total warning cycle duration (30 seconds).
