## Basis
5.2.1.31.1.
The signal shall be activated by the application of the service braking system as
follows:
10/ At the time of type approval, compliance with this requirement shall be confirmed by the
vehicle manufacturer.
11/ During a "selective braking" event, the function may change to "automatically commanded
braking".
12/ This requirement shall not apply until the Standard ISO 11992 has been amended to
include a message "illuminate stop lamps".
