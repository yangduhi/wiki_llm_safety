# S5.1.2 If the seat back and the seat bench are attached to the vehicle by different attachments, attach to each componen

## Statement
S5.1.2 If the seat back and the seat bench are attached to the vehicle by different attachments, attach to each component a fixture capable of transmitting a force to that component. Apply forces, in newtons, equal to 20 times the mass of the seat back in kilograms multiplied by 9.8 m/s 2 horizontally through the center of gravity of the seat back, as shown in Figure 2 and apply forces, in newtons, equal to 20 times the mass of the seat bench in kilograms multiplied by 9.8 m/s 2 horizontally through the center of gravity of the seat bench, as shown in Figure 3.
