# S5.2 The steering control system shall be so constructed that no components or attachments, including horn actuating mec

## Statement
S5.2 The steering control system shall be so constructed that no components or attachments, including horn actuating mechanisms and trim hardware, can catch the driver's clothing or jewelry during normal driving maneuvers.

S5.2 The steering control system shall be so constructed that no components or attachments, including horn actuating mechanisms and trim hardware, can catch the driver's clothing or jewelry during normal driving maneuvers.
Note: The term jewelry refers to watches, rings, and bracelets without loosely attached or dangling members.
