# (2) The minimum value for the burner monitor temperature during the engulfing fire stage (Tmin ENG ) shall be calculated

## Statement
(2) The minimum value for the burner monitor temperature during the engulfing fire stage (Tmin ENG ) shall be calculated by subtracting 50 °C from the 60-second rolling average of the average of the three burner monitor temperatures during the engulfing fire stage of the pre-test checkout. If the resultant Tmin ENG exceeds 800 °C, Tmin ENG shall be 800 °C.
