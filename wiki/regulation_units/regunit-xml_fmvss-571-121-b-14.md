# (b) Notwithstanding S5.4.3, neither front axle brake of a bus or a truck other than a truck-tractor is subject to the re

## Statement
(b) Notwithstanding S5.4.3, neither front axle brake of a bus or a truck other than a truck-tractor is subject to the requirement set forth in S5.4.3 prohibiting the service line air pressure from being less than 20 lb/in 2 for a brake not subject to the control of an antilock system or 12 lb/in 2 for a brake subject to the control of an antilock system.
