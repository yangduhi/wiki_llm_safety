## Basis
13.11.
As long as there are no requirements forbidding side-facing seats in their
national requirements at the time of acceding to this Regulation, Contracting
Parties may continue to allow the fitting of side-facing seats for the purpose
of national approval and in this case these bus categories cannot be type
approved under this Regulation
