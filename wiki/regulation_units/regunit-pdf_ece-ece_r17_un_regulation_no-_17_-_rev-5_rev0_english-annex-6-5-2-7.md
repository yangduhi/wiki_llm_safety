## Basis
5.2.7.
After the tests, the displacement systems intended for permitting or
facilitating the access of occupants shall be in working order; they shall be
capable, at least once, of being unlocked and shall permit the displacement of
the seat or the part of the seat for which they are intended.
Any other displacement systems, as well as adjustment systems and their
locking systems are not required to be in working order.
In the case of seats provided with head restraints, the strength of the seat-
back and of its locking devices is deemed to meet the requirements set out in
paragraph 6.2. when, after testing in accordance with paragraph 6.4.3.6
below., no breakage of the seat or seat-back has occurred: otherwise, it shall
be shown that the seat is capable of meeting the test requirements set out in
paragraph 6.2. below.
In the case of seats (benches) with more places to sit than head restraints, the
test described in paragraph 6.2. below shall be carried out.
