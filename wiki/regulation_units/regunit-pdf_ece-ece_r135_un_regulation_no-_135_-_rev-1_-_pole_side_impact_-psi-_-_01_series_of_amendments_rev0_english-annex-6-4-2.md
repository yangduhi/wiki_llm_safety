## Basis
4.2.
The initial mass of hydrogen in the storage system can be calculated as
follows:
Po' = Po x 288 / (273 + T0)
ρo' = –0.0027 x (P0')2 + 0.75 x P0' + 0.5789
Mo = ρo' x VCHSS
