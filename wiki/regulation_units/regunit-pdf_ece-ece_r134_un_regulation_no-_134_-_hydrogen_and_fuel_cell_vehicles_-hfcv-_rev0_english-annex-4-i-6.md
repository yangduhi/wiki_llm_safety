## Basis
(i)
An equilibrium (steady state temperature) hold is established
for one hour at 1.5 times the rated voltage;
(ii)
The voltage is increased to two times the rated voltage or 60
volts, whichever is less, and held for one minute;
(iii)
Any failure shall not result in external leakage, open valve or
unsafe conditions such as smoke, fire or melting.
The minimum opening voltage at NWP and room temperature shall be
less than or equal to 9 V for a 12 V system and less than or equal to 18
V for a 24 V system.
