## Basis
8.4.4.
The competent authority may carry out any test prescribed in this Regulation.
The normal frequency of inspections authorized by the competent authority
shall be one per year. In cases where unsatisfactory results are found during
one of these inspections, the competent authority shall ensure that all
necessary steps are taken to restore conformity of production as rapidly as
possible.
