# S8.7 A vehicle produced by more than one manufacturer shall be attributed to any one of the vehicle's manufacturers spec

## Statement
S8.7 A vehicle produced by more than one manufacturer shall be attributed to any one of the vehicle's manufacturers specified by an express written contract, reported to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration under 49 CFR part 585, between the manufacturer so specified and the manufacturer to which the vehicle would otherwise be attributed under S8.5.
