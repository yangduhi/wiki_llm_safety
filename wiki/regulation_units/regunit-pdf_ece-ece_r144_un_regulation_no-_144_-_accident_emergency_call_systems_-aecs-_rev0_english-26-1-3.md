## Basis
26.1.3.
The installation of the AECD shall be such to obtain reception of the GNSS
signal and access to a PLMN.
The applicant shall provide the relevant information about the PLMN and
GNSS receiver to which the AECS is intended.
The installation and orientation of the AECD and its components shall
correspond to the AECD approval in a vehicle frontal impact configuration.
