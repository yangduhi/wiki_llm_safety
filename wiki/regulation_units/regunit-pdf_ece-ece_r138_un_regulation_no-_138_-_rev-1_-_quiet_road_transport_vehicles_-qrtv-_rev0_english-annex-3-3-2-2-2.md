## Basis
3.2.2.
Battery state of charge
If so equipped, propulsion batteries shall have a state-of-charge sufficiently
high to enable all key functionalities according to the manufacturer's
specifications. Propulsion batteries shall be within their component-
temperature window to enable all key functionalities that could reduce
vehicle sound emissions. Any other type of rechargeable energy storage
system shall be ready to operate during the test.
