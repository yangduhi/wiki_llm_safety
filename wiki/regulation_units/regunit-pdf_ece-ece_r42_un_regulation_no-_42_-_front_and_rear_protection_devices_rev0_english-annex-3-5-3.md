## Basis
5.3.
Notice of approval or of refusal of approval of a vehicle type
pursuant to this Regulation shall be communicated to the Parties to
the Agreement which apply this Regulation by means of a form
conforming to the model in annex 1 to this Regulation and of
dimensioned drawings of the vehicle structure supplied by the
applicant for approval in a format not exceeding A4 (210 x 297 mm)
or folded to that format and on an appropriate scale.
