# (g) Buckle latch. The buckle latch of a seat belt assembly when tested by the procedure specified in S5.2(g) shall not f

## Statement
(g) Buckle latch. The buckle latch of a seat belt assembly when tested by the procedure specified in S5.2(g) shall not fail, nor gall or wear to an extent that normal latching and unlatching is impaired, and a metal-to-metal buckle shall separate when in any position of partial engagement by a force of not more than 22 N.
