# S5.4 Reflectivity. When the red reflex reflective material on the warning device is tested in accordance with S6.2, both

## Statement

When the red reflex reflective material on the warning device is tested in accordance with S6.2, both before and after the warning device has been conditioned in accordance with S6.1, its total candlepower per incident foot candle shall be not less than the values specified in Table I for each of the listed entrance angles.
