# (a) Add-on child restraint systems. For an add-on child restraint system other than a car bed, apply the specified force

## Statement
(a) Add-on child restraint systems. For an add-on child restraint system other than a car bed, apply the specified force by pulling the sling horizontally and parallel to the SORL of the standard seat assembly. For a car bed, apply the force by pulling the sling vertically.
