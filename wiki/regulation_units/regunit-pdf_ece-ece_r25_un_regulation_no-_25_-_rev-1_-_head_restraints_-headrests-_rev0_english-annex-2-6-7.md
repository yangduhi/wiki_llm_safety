## Basis
6.7.
The width of the head restraint shall be such as to provide
suitable support for the head of a person normally seated. In the
plane of measurement of width defined in paragraph 7.3 below, the
head restraint shall cover an area extending not less than 85 mm to
each side of the plane of symmetry of the seat for which the head
restraint is intended, that distance being measured as prescribed
in paragraph 7.3.
