## Basis
6.1.
To enable the simplified test described in Annex 7 to be carried out, the
deceleration time history of the structure shall be determined on the basis of
the value of the longitudinal accelerometers at the base of the "B" pillar on
the struck side of the vehicle with a CFC of 180 by means of data channels
corresponding to the requirements set out in Annex 8;
