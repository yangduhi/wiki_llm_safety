## Basis
2.6.
"Reference line" means a straight line which, either on a test
manikin having the weight and dimensions of a fiftieth percentile
adult male or on a test manikin having identical characteristics,
passes through the joint of the leg with the pelvis and the joint
of the neck with the thorax. On the manikin reproduced in annex 3
to this Regulation, for determining the H point of the seat, the
reference line is that shown in fig. 1 in the appendix to that
annex;
